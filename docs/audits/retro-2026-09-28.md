## Proposals

Each is `optimization`; the architect shapes them, this report does not.
The `retro` skill does not name the `docs/README.md` row a report needs
(`test_every_named_audit_document_has_a_readme_table_row`); this one
added it.

1. **`dev_retro.py` keys a ledger line by its own class field.** 7 of 15
   ledger lines read unclassed though each carries one; "none reported" is no
   finding. Friction cells, 117 of 117 unclassed, need a key of their
   own (the agent and the command the cell names), or leave the count.
2. **The records writers queue instead of cancelling.** Chain the three
   adopt jobs with `needs:`, or one job writes all three records. A
   cancelled adopt drops a measurement silently, and `UX-1000`'s
   `test_it_shares_the_records_concurrency_group` asserts the group, not
   that no writer is dropped.
3. **A declared guard field closes the `coverage` class.** 9 of 15 ledger
   lines this week are "guard reads nothing" (`coverage`); `UX-1000`'s
   page reads 366 / 567 with 245 inferred from prose. One `Guard:` field
   at filing makes both exact.

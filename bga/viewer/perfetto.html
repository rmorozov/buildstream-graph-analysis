Handing the timeline to Perfetto

Opening ui.perfetto.dev…

The trace is sent tab to tab and processed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: ui.perfetto.dev is a static site with no server side to send a trace to. It looks like an upload, which is why this says so.

If nothing happens, your browser blocked the pop-up. — or open ui.perfetto.dev and drag timeline.json.gz into it.
